# Third-party game art

## Universal Animation Library (Standard) — 3D hero skeleton and animations

- Creator: Quaternius.
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain), as supplied with the pack the project owner provided.
- Included file: `public/assets/hero/hero_rig.glb` (about 1.5 MB). It holds only the pack's 65-bone skeleton and 16 animation clips (idle, talking idle, walk, jog, sprint, sword attack, roll, hit, death, jump), trimmed and meshopt-compressed with gltf-transform. The pack's mannequin mesh is not used.
- Use: `src/game3d/render/hero/`. The hero's body, face, hair, clothing, sword and accessories are modelled in code and skinned to this skeleton, so the wardrobe still recolours and equips every part.
- No attribution is required under CC0; the creator is credited here as a courtesy.

## KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers 1.0 — the chibi hero (v2.8)

- Creator: Kay Lousberg (KayKit). Source: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0), also on [itch.io](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers).
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)**. Evidence checked in this project: the pack's own `LICENSE.txt` (copied unchanged to `public/assets/chibi/LICENSE.txt`) states "Creative Commons Zero, CC0" and that the content is free to use in personal, educational and *commercial* projects, with credit optional. The itch.io page and the Godot Asset Library listing for the pack also give CC0.
- Scope of the CC0 grant: the **free** "Adventurers" pack only. The creator also sells an "Extra"/premium tier and other packs under their own terms; nothing from those is used here, and none should be added without reading their licence.
- Included file: `public/assets/chibi/chibi_kit.glb` (about 1.3 MB), generated by `tools/chibi-kit/build-chibi-kit.mjs`. It merges the heads, bodies, arms, legs, hats, helmet, capes, weapons and shields of the pack's Knight, Barbarian, Mage and Rogue characters onto their shared skeleton, keeps 45 of the pack's 76 animation clips, removes IK/scale tracks, and quantises and meshopt-compresses the geometry. The four colour atlases are embedded unchanged (the game recolours copies of them at runtime).
- Use: `src/game3d/render/chibi/`, `src/game3d/cosmetics/`. The leaf crown and glow lantern props are still built in code.
- No attribution is required under CC0; the creator is credited here and in `public/assets/chibi/README.txt` as a courtesy (the game has no credits screen yet).
- Note: CC0 is the creator's own declaration. Nothing here checks that the creator owned every part of the pack; the project accepts the creator's dedication as published.

## 3D world art (v2.3)

Everything else in the 3D game (trees, rocks, grass, flowers, mushrooms, the village, the ruins, the Moon Gate, the Elder, the Rune Stone, the petals, the enemies, the Ancient Tree, the mountains and clouds) is generated by code in `src/game3d/render/env/` and `src/game3d/render/hero/` (the hero's own body now comes from the KayKit kit above). No third-party meshes or textures are used for them, so nothing else needs a licence.

## Audio

There is no third-party audio. Every sound (music, ambience, effects) is synthesised at runtime in `src/game/audio/GameAudio.ts` with the Web Audio API: oscillators, filtered noise and envelopes. No samples or audio files are bundled or loaded, so there is nothing to license or attribute.
